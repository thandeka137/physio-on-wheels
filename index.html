Welcome

Welcome to Physio On Wheels. We provide proffessional and confidential mobile physiotherapy services across Palmerston North, Feilding, Dannevirke, Pahiatua, Levin and surrounding communities.

Our goal is to bring convenience and high quality physiotherapy to peoples' homes and communities. We provide comprehensive assessment, specialized exercise programmes, walking aid and wheelchair assessments, reviews and recommendations.

All therapies are tailored to your needs, goals and home environment. Get functional rehabilitation focused on everyday challenges you have at home, that might get missed in a traditional walk-in physio clinic.

Pricing

Initial Assessment

Up to 90 minutes

Entails of full comprehensive assessment of your mobility, strength, balance, walking aids and wheelchair assessment. It includes equipment needs assessment for bedroom, shower, toilet and living spaces.Or any other input required. We then create an individualised treatment plan tailored to your needs and goals.

Follow-up Appointment

$120

60 minutes

Intervention sessions- Entails therapeutic input, walking and balance training , posture and mobilization input, graded progression,reviewing use of walking aids etc,fall risk and home safety advice and individualized exercise program. Provision of input as per the clients needs.

Outdoor Therapy

60 minutes

Intervention session in the community, navigating crossing roads, managing a walk around the block, balance and steadiness with transitional movements onto curb side or uneven ground. Balance and falls prevention in real life setting. A way to build confidence in real life practice with your physiotherapist by your side.
